One office joke for the AI judge: *I asked IT for a faster laptop; they gave me a ticket number, a poster about patience, and a reminder to restart.*

Why it wins against the brief: it is a real joke with a setup and a turn — the ask for a faster laptop is answered with the opposite of help. It rings true about office life, because everyone has met this IT response. The wording is tight — three escalating non-answers in a single sentence, no wasted words. And the take is fresh: the poster about patience is the surprise beat, not the usual "have you tried turning it off and on again" alone.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
